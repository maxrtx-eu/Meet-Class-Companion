MEET CLASS COMPANION - INSTALLATION GUIDE
==========================================

Two roles. Students install a Chrome extension. Professors install nothing:
they use a web page.


------------------------------------------------------------
FOR STUDENTS
------------------------------------------------------------

You need:
  - A computer (not a phone or tablet)
  - Google Chrome 120 or newer
  - The CLASS CODE from your professor (8 characters, like K7M2QX9P)

INSTALL (about 2 minutes)

  1. Unzip the file you were sent into a folder you will KEEP
     (for example Documents\MeetCompanion). Do not delete or move it later,
     because Chrome reads the extension from that folder.
  2. In Chrome, open:  chrome://extensions
  3. Switch on "Developer mode" (top right).
  4. Click "Load unpacked" and select the folder that contains manifest.json.
  5. Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin
     "Meet Class Companion" (a chalkboard with a yellow tick).
  6. If you already had a Google Meet tab open, refresh it.

USE IT

  1. Open your Meet link. In the waiting room, a window asks:
     "Your choice: share an attention log for this class?"
  2. Type the class code in the box and press "Link class". The text updates
     and now names your professor and class.
  3. Enter your name and your student number.
  4. Read the text, then choose "Turn on the attention log" or
     "No thanks, do not track me". Both are fine. Nothing is recorded or
     uploaded until you choose.
  5. A small badge in the top-left of Meet shows "Attention log on" or "off".
  6. Notes tool (works whether or not you accept): click the extension icon
     to open the side panel. Type notes, press "Capture slide" to screenshot
     what the professor is presenting (you crop it before it is saved), and
     export to Markdown or PDF. Your notes and screenshots stay on your
     computer and are never uploaded.

WHAT THE LOG RECORDS (only if you accept)

  - When you switch tabs or windows, when Chrome loses focus, and when you leave
  - Whether you answered the "Are you still there?" box, and how fast
  - When you are taking notes (the time only, never what you wrote)

  It cannot see which other apps or websites you use. The "My log" tab in the
  side panel shows exactly what has been recorded.

EDITING YOUR NAME OR STUDENT NUMBER (even after you said yes)

  You can change them at any time, whether the log is on, off or not set yet.
  Two ways:

  A. In Meet: click the small badge in the top-left ("Attention log on").
     Under "Your details", change your name or student number and press
     "Save details".

  B. In the side panel: click the extension icon, open the "Privacy" tab,
     change the fields under "Your details", and press "Save details".

  Good to know: new details apply from your NEXT class. A class that is
  already in progress keeps the name and number it started with, because
  records already written are never rewritten. If you fixed a typo, tell your
  professor which class it affected.

CHANGING YOUR MIND OR YOUR CLASS

  - Stop sharing: click the badge, then "Stop sharing my log". Logging stops
    at once and your record gets one line:
    "No tracking record. Student declined tracking."
  - Turn it back on: click the badge, then "Turn on the attention log".
  - Different class: click the badge, then "Change class" and enter the new
    code. You will be asked for your choice again, because a different
    professor would see your log.

UPDATING THE EXTENSION

  Put the new files in the same folder (replace the old ones), then press the
  reload arrow on the extension at chrome://extensions.


------------------------------------------------------------
FOR PROFESSORS (nothing to install)
------------------------------------------------------------

SET UP YOUR CLASS (once)

  1. Open https://class-companion-ce085.web.app in any browser.
  2. Sign in with Google.
  3. Under "Create a class", fill in the class name, your name (students see
     it in the consent form), your email, and how many days to keep the data
     (default 120). UNTICK "Email me a report after each meeting".
  4. Press "Create class and get the class code". You will see an 8-character
     code.
  5. Send the code to your students. They enter it once in the extension.

DURING AND AFTER A MEETING

  1. Run your Google Meet call as usual. Students with the extension join it
     as normal.
  2. Open the dashboard, click your class, then press "Refresh". Your meeting
     appears in the "Meetings" table (one row per meeting and day).
  3. Click the meeting. You get a table with one row per student:
       Engaged          Time on the class tab (includes note-taking).
       Confirmed idle   Time after an "Are you still there?" box went
                        unanswered.
       Away             Tab switched, Chrome unfocused, or screen locked.
       Not monitored    The extension was not running.
       Log integrity    "Intact" means the log was not edited after it was
                        written.
  4. Click a student to see their timeline and every event, with two download
     buttons: "Events CSV" and "Detailed file (.json)".
  5. "Download summary CSV" gives the whole class in one spreadsheet.
  6. Under the table, the dashboard lists students who linked the class but
     have no record for this meeting. It cannot tell whether they were
     absent, on a phone, or not using the extension.

READING IT FAIRLY

  Treat the numbers as something to discuss with a student, not a verdict.
  Picture-in-picture, a second monitor and a locked screen have their own
  rows and are never counted for or against anyone. A student who declined
  shows as "Declined tracking".

IF A STUDENT'S UPLOAD FAILED

  Ask them for the file from "My log" > "Hand-in file (.json)". Then open
  report\professor-report-viewer.html from the extension folder (double-click
  it, it works offline) and drop the file in.
